ADVANCED NEUROLOGICAL NURSING CARE

A Critical Analysis of Acute Stroke Management: Cerebral Edema Management and Neuromonitoring in the Context of Healthcare Delivery in Timor-Leste

This academic work examines advanced neurological nursing care in acute stroke management, with particular attention to cerebral edema, neuromonitoring, clinical decision-making, patient safety, and the challenges of healthcare delivery in Timor-Leste.

Abstract

Acute stroke is a major clinical challenge requiring timely recognition, coordinated multidisciplinary intervention, and continuous neurological assessment. Cerebral edema may contribute to secondary brain injury and neurological deterioration, making early detection and appropriate monitoring essential components of acute stroke care.

This work critically examines the principles of advanced neurological nursing in acute stroke management, focusing on cerebral edema surveillance, neurological assessment, neuromonitoring, clinical escalation, patient safety, and evidence-informed nursing practice. It also considers the practical implications of delivering specialized care in Timor-Leste, where resource availability, workforce development, referral pathways, and access to advanced monitoring may influence clinical outcomes.

By connecting clinical knowledge with the realities of healthcare delivery, this publication emphasizes the role of nurses in recognizing deterioration, communicating clinical changes, supporting coordinated treatment, and preserving patient dignity. It aims to contribute to professional education, clinical reflection, and the continuing development of neurological nursing services in Timor-Leste.
