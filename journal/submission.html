1. Submission Guidelines

The International Journal of Health and Social Sciences (IJHSS), published by Lusofonia Press, welcomes original scholarly contributions in health sciences and the social sciences.

Authors are invited to submit manuscripts that demonstrate academic rigor, originality, clarity of argument, and relevance to the journal's scope.

2. Types of Manuscripts

IJHSS considers scholarly contributions including:

  • Original Research Articles
  • Review Articles
  • Conceptual and Theoretical Papers
  • Case Studies
  • Research Essays in Health Sciences and the Social Sciences

The suitability of each submission is determined through editorial assessment.

3. Manuscript Preparation

Authors should prepare manuscripts according to the following principles:

  • Manuscripts must be written in clear academic English or another language expressly accepted by the editorial office.
  • The manuscript must be original and must not be under consideration by another journal.
  • The title, abstract, and keywords should clearly represent the content of the manuscript.
  • References and citations must be consistent and complete.
  • Tables and figures must be numbered and accompanied by appropriate titles or captions.

4. Title Page

A separate title page should include the manuscript title, full names of all authors, institutional affiliations, and the corresponding author's email address.

5. Anonymous Manuscript

Authors should provide a manuscript file that does not contain identifying information if anonymous peer review is requested or required by the editorial office. Identifying information should be provided separately on the title page.

6. Required Declarations

Authors should include the following statements where applicable:

  • Author Contributions: A description of each author's contribution to the work.
  • Funding: Disclosure of financial support or a statement that no specific funding was received.
  • Conflict of Interest: Disclosure of any potential conflicts of interest or a statement that none exist.
  • Ethics Approval: Relevant ethical approval and informed consent statements for research involving human participants, where applicable.

7. Editorial Assessment and Peer Review

Submitted manuscripts are subject to an initial editorial assessment to determine their relevance, originality, academic quality, and compliance with the journal's submission requirements.

Manuscripts considered suitable may proceed to external peer review. Editorial decisions are based on scholarly merit and the reviewers' recommendations.

8. How to Submit

At this stage, manuscripts should be submitted by email to the IJHSS Editorial Office.

Email submission:
editorial.lusofon@gmail.com

Please include the manuscript title and the corresponding author's name in your email. Attach the manuscript and a separate title page.

9. Publication Fees and Open Access Policy

The International Journal of Health and Social Sciences (IJHSS), published by Lusofonia Press, does not charge authors any submission fees, article processing charges (APCs), or publication fees.

This initial no-fee policy reflects the journal's commitment to equitable scholarly communication and to encouraging academic contributions from researchers across countries, institutions, and diverse academic backgrounds.

All submitted manuscripts are evaluated according to the journal's editorial standards and peer-review procedures. Editorial decisions are based on scholarly quality, originality, relevance, and academic integrity, regardless of the authors' financial circumstances.

Future Policy Review

The no-fee policy will remain in effect during the journal's initial development. Lusofonia Press may review this policy in the future if and when IJHSS achieves inclusion in Scopus. Any proposed changes will be evaluated carefully, announced transparently, and communicated to authors in advance.

Until an official revision is announced, all manuscript submissions, peer-review processes, and publications remain free of charge.

10. Editorial Correspondence

All communications concerning manuscript submission, editorial assessment, peer review, and publication should be directed to the IJHSS Editorial Office.
