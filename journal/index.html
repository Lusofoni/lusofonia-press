About the Journal

The International Journal of Health and Social Sciences (IJHSS) is an academic journal established to promote high-quality scholarly research, critical reflection, and interdisciplinary dialogue in health and social sciences.

Published by Lusofonia Press, IJHSS welcomes original contributions from researchers, academics, healthcare professionals, educators, and scholars from different countries and academic traditions.

The journal seeks to strengthen the relationship between scientific knowledge, professional practice, social responsibility, and human development.

Aims and Scope

IJHSS provides an interdisciplinary platform for research addressing contemporary challenges in health, society, education, and human development.

Health and Nursing Sciences

Nursing practice, clinical care, maternal and child health, community health, and healthcare quality.

Public Health

Epidemiology, disease prevention, health promotion, health policy, and health systems.

Health Ethics and Philosophy

Bioethics, professional ethics, philosophy of care, human dignity, and ethical responsibilities.

Education and Social Sciences

Educational research, sociology, psychology, social development, culture, and public policy.

Types of Contributions

  • Original Research Articles
  • Narrative and Integrative Literature Reviews
  • Systematic Reviews and Scoping Reviews
  • Case Studies and Case Reports

All submissions must demonstrate academic relevance, methodological transparency, and appropriate ethical standards.

Peer Review Policy

IJHSS is committed to a fair, independent, and academically rigorous peer-review process. Manuscripts may be evaluated by qualified reviewers with relevant expertise.

Manuscripts will first undergo editorial screening for relevance, originality, academic quality, and compliance with the journal's submission requirements.

Suitable manuscripts will be evaluated by independent qualified reviewers. Editorial decisions will be based on scholarly merit, methodological quality, relevance, and reviewers' recommendations.

Publication Ethics

IJHSS is committed to responsible scholarly publishing, academic integrity, transparency, and the protection of research participants.

  • Originality and prevention of plagiarism.
  • Disclosure of conflicts of interest.
  • Transparent authorship and contributions.
  • Research ethics approval where applicable.
  • Informed consent and protection of participants.
  • Responsible handling of corrections and retractions.

The journal will develop its publication ethics procedures with reference to recognized international scholarly publishing standards, including COPE guidance.

Open Access Policy

IJHSS intends to provide immediate open access to published scholarly content, supporting the global exchange of knowledge and wider access to academic research.

IJHSS does not charge submission fees, article processing charges (APCs), or publication fees during its initial publication stage. Any future changes to this policy will be communicated transparently and in advance.

Licensing, copyright, and digital archiving policies will be clearly stated in the journal's publication guidelines.
