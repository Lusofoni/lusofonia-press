Editorial

Advancing Health, Social Equity, and Interdisciplinary Knowledge

The inaugural issue of the International Journal of Health and Social Sciences (IJHSS) marks an important step in establishing an interdisciplinary platform for scholarly dialogue, research dissemination, and knowledge advancement across health and social sciences.

Published by Lusofonia Press, IJHSS was established to encourage rigorous academic inquiry into contemporary challenges affecting health, healthcare systems, society, human well-being, equity, and sustainable development. The journal recognises that complex societal and health challenges require interdisciplinary perspectives, critical reflection, and the integration of knowledge across academic and professional boundaries.

This first issue brings together scholarly contributions addressing community health systems, population health, nursing ethics, digital transformation, equity, resilience, and related interdisciplinary concerns. Collectively, these contributions reflect the importance of connecting theoretical perspectives, professional practice, health-system development, and social realities.

The journal welcomes original research, conceptual studies, reviews, and scholarly perspectives that contribute to academic knowledge and meaningful professional and societal advancement. We are committed to responsible scholarly publishing, editorial integrity, transparent publication practices, and the dissemination of knowledge through an open-access approach.

The launch of Volume 1, Issue 1 represents both an achievement and a beginning. We hope IJHSS will grow into a meaningful international platform for researchers, academics, healthcare professionals, policymakers, and scholars committed to advancing knowledge and improving human well-being.

We extend our sincere appreciation to the authors, reviewers, editorial contributors, and colleagues whose commitment and scholarly work have made this inaugural issue possible.
