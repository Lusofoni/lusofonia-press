CHRONIC DISEASES AND NONCOMMUNICABLE DISEASES IN TIMOR-LESTE

Global and Local Trends, Epidemiology, Disease Burden, and Health System Challenges

This book examines chronic diseases and noncommunicable diseases in Timor-Leste through the perspectives of global and local trends, epidemiology, disease burden, and health system challenges. It provides a context for understanding the importance of prevention, early detection, continuity of care, health equity, and the development of resilient health services.
